1. Purpose of This Disclosure

In the interest of transparency, this page discloses where and how artificial intelligence (AI) is used on Bancostore.

2. AI-Generated Imagery

Some decorative and marketing imagery on the Platform — such as hero banners and lifestyle photography used on informational pages like About and Home — may be AI-generated rather than real photographs. These images are used purely for visual atmosphere and are not depictions of real Bancostore staff, distributors, customers, or premises.

Actual product photos are not AI-generated. Every image shown on a product listing or product detail page is a real photograph of the product provided by Bancostore or its suppliers. If you are ever unsure whether a specific image is a real product photo, please ask us through our Contact page before purchasing.

3. No AI-Generated Advice

Bancostore does not use an AI chatbot or automated system to give you financial, legal, tax, or medical advice, or to make binding decisions about your account, orders, KYC verification, or commissions. Decisions that affect your account (such as KYC approval or withdrawal approval) are made by our human admin team, in line with our documented policies — never by an automated system alone.

4. Why We Disclose This

We believe you deserve to know when what you're looking at isn't a real photograph. This disclosure will be kept up to date as our use of AI on the Platform changes.

5. Changes to This Disclaimer

We may update this AI Disclaimer as our use of AI-generated content changes. The "Last updated" date above always reflects the most recent version.

6. Contact Us

Questions about AI-generated content on the Platform? Reach us through our Contact page.
