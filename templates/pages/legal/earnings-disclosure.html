1. Purpose of This Disclosure

This page explains, honestly and in plain language, how distributors can earn on Bancostore, what participation costs, and — most importantly — that no income is ever guaranteed. Please read this in full before registering as a distributor.

2. No Guaranteed Income

Becoming a Bancostore distributor gives you the opportunity to earn commissions — it is not a job, a salary, or an investment with a promised return. Your results depend entirely on your own effort, skill, the size and activity of your network, and market conditions, all of which vary widely from person to person. Most distributors earn modest amounts or none at all; a small number may earn significantly more. Past or reported results of any individual distributor are not typical and are not a promise of what you will earn.

3. How Distributors Can Earn

Bancostore's commission structure has three parts, all funded by real product sales, not by recruitment fees alone:

  • Direct Referral Bonus — a one-time bonus paid to you when someone you personally sponsor completes their starter pack purchase.
  • Binary Bonus — paid periodically based on matched sales volume (PV) on your weaker leg of your binary tree, up to a weekly cap. Unmatched volume can carry forward for a limited time.
  • Matching Bonus — a percentage of the Binary Bonus earned by distributors in your personal sponsor line, up to a limited number of levels (more levels unlock at higher ranks).

Exact rates, caps, and eligibility thresholds (such as the minimum monthly personal PV required) are business settings that Bancostore's admin team may adjust over time — the current rates are always shown in your distributor dashboard, not fixed numbers printed here.

4. Costs of Participation

Joining as a distributor requires a one-time registration fee and a starter pack purchase, both shown clearly before you pay. These are real costs, not guaranteed to be recovered through commissions. New distributors have a cooling-off period (shown at signup and on your dashboard) during which membership can be cancelled for a partial refund, minus a processing fee.

5. KYC and Payout Requirements

Before your first withdrawal, you must complete identity verification (KYC). Withdrawals are subject to minimum and maximum amounts, a processing schedule, and withholding tax deducted in line with Ghana Revenue Authority (GRA) requirements — all shown in your dashboard before you submit a withdrawal request.

6. Not a Pyramid Scheme

Bancostore sells real, tangible products to real customers. Commissions are tied to product sales volume moving through the binary tree, not simply to recruiting new distributors. You are never required to buy products solely to qualify for a bonus in a way disconnected from genuine retail or personal use.

7. Examples Are Illustrative Only

Any example numbers shown anywhere on the Platform (in onboarding materials, dashboard explanations, or elsewhere) are for illustration only, to help you understand how the formulas work. They are not typical results and are not a promise of what you personally will earn.

8. Do Your Own Research

Before registering as a distributor or paying for a starter pack, we encourage you to review this disclosure carefully, ask questions through our Contact page, and make your own informed decision about whether this opportunity is right for you.
