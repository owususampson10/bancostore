This guide explains, in plain language, how to shop on Bancostore and how to become a distributor — from registering to earning your first commission. It isn't a policy document; for the legal terms governing your account, see our Terms of Use and Earnings & Income Disclosure.

1. Shopping as a Customer

You don't need an account to shop. Browse products, add items to your cart, and check out as a guest — your cart is kept for you as you browse.

  1. Browse the Shop page, or a product's category, and add items to your cart.
  2. Go to checkout, enter your delivery details (or choose free pickup), and pay securely with Paystack — by card or Mobile Money.
  3. Once payment is confirmed, your order is placed. You'll see live order status updates: Pending, Confirmed, Processing, Dispatched, and Delivered.

Creating an account lets you save delivery addresses, keep a wishlist, view your full order history, and leave a review once an order has been delivered. See our Returns, Refunds & Shipping Policy for delivery fees and order details.

2. Becoming a Distributor

Becoming a distributor means joining Bancostore's referral network as an Independent Representative (IR), with your own permanent IR ID and the ability to earn commissions. It happens in a fixed order:

  1. Register. Fill in your details on the Become a Distributor page. You'll need a sponsoring distributor's IR ID to register — usually pre-filled automatically if you followed someone's referral link.
  2. Pay the registration fee — a one-time fee of GHS {{ registration_fee|intcomma }}, paid securely through Paystack.
  3. Choose and pay for a starter pack. This is what activates your account and places you in the binary tree:
    • Starter Pack A — GHS {{ starter_pack_a_price|intcomma }}, {{ starter_pack_a_pv|intcomma }} PV, starting rank {{ starter_pack_a_rank|title }}.
    • Starter Pack B — GHS {{ starter_pack_b_price|intcomma }}, {{ starter_pack_b_pv|intcomma }} PV, starting rank {{ starter_pack_b_rank|title }}.
  4. Complete identity verification (KYC). You'll be guided through a quick, secure process: photos of the front and back of your ID, plus a live selfie, all handled on our verification partner's own page.
  5. Wait for approval. Once our team reviews and approves your verification, you're issued a permanent IR ID — this is what makes you a fully active distributor able to refer others and earn commissions.

Changed your mind shortly after joining? New distributors have a short window to cancel their membership for a partial refund — see our Returns, Refunds & Shipping Policy for the cooling-off cancellation window.

3. How Sponsoring Works

There's no separate "become a sponsor" step — it happens automatically. Once you have your IR ID, your distributor dashboard shows your own personal referral link. Share it with anyone (WhatsApp, social media, or in person):

  • Anyone who registers through your link automatically has your IR ID filled in as their sponsor — and, once they arrive from a real referral link, that field is locked so it can't be accidentally cleared.
  • You become their sponsor: you're credited as their recruiter, and you earn a Direct Referral Bonus when they purchase their starter pack.
  • They're also placed into the binary tree on your side — though for tree-balancing reasons, they may land a level or two below you rather than directly underneath, if your own immediate positions are already filled.

  • Binary Bonus ({{ binary_bonus_rate }}%) — earned on your weaker leg's PV (Personal Volume), calculated automatically on a regular schedule.
  • Matching Bonus ({{ matching_bonus_rate }}%) — earned on your downline's binary bonus earnings. All your earnings — wallet balance, this week's earnings, team size, PV, and rank — are visible on your distributor dashboard at a glance.

5. Requesting a Withdrawal

Once you've earned a balance in your wallet, you can request a withdrawal from your dashboard:

  • Your KYC must be approved, and you must have set a payout destination (a Mobile Money number and network) in your payout settings.
  • Withdrawal requests must be between GHS {{ min_withdrawal_amount|intcomma }} and GHS {{ max_withdrawal_amount|intcomma }}, and can be made {{ withdrawal_frequency }}.
  • Approved withdrawals are processed on {{ withdrawal_day|title }}s.
  • A withholding tax of {{ withholding_tax_rate }}% is deducted from every withdrawal and remitted to the Ghana Revenue Authority (GRA) — the exact tax and net amount are always shown to you before you submit a request.
  • Requests are reviewed and approved by our team before payout.
